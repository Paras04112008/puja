FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers for families planning a traditional puja.

How can I book a puja?

Use the booking/enquiry form or WhatsApp button. Share the service you are interested in, preferred date and basic contact details.

Can I ask which puja is suitable for my situation?

Yes. The enquiry form can be used to request guidance before selecting a service.

How early should I enquire?

For the final client website, add the preferred advance-booking period based on actual availability and ceremony requirements.

Are puja materials provided?

This depends on the service. The final version should clearly state which samagri is included and which items the devotee needs to arrange.

Can the puja be performed on a specific date?

Dates are subject to ritual requirements and availability. Send an enquiry with your preferred date.

How do I get directions?

The contact page can include a Google Maps embed once the client's verified location is available.
